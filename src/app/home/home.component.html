I'm Jernej Klancic, a Staff Software Engineer working on AI tooling at SAP LeanIX. My love for technology started with a Commodore 64 as a kid and never let up — it's taken me through software engineering and, for a couple of years, engineering management, while I've lived and worked across Slovenia, Germany, and Canada.

Today, I build the infrastructure underneath our MCP server, going from tool deprecation, progressive tool discovery, and whatever's newest in the MCP ecosystem. This allows other teams to focus entirely on building great tools instead of building the plumbing beneath them. It's not so different from managing engineers: I still care more about making other people effective than about the code itself, and I keep coding daily to stay sharp.
